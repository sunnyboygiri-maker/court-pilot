CourtPilot · UI redesign · Phase 1

What gets in a lawyer's way, and three ways forward

An audit of every current screen on a phone (375 px) and a laptop (1280 px), ranked by how much each problem costs a lawyer on a court day. Then three visual directions to choose from. Nothing in the app has been changed yet.

Screens reviewed: login, Home, All cases, case page, Add case (6 tabs), search results, Settings, My courts Data: the demo account (Adv. Priya Sharma, 8 cases)Date: 5 Oct 2026

The 15 problems, most costly first

  1. 01

    Missed hearings sit at the bottom of Home High

    Home

    Two urgent cases whose hearings passed on 2 and 3 Oct with no next date listed appear last on Home, under "Waiting for next date", below every hearing up to 20 Nov. The top of the page says "1 today, 0 tomorrow" and looks calm.

    Fix: a "Needs attention" strip at the top of Today: hearing passed without a new date, judge on leave, new order not yet opened.

  2. 02

    What a lawyer needs in the corridor isn't on Home High

    Home

    Item number, court room, judge, cause-list status and the VC link now exist, but only inside each case page. In court you have to open every case to find your item.

    Fix: Today rows lead with the item number and court room, then the case title, stage and a Join VC button.

  3. 03

    No way to see the week or month ahead High

    Missing screen

    Home counts "this week" but there is no calendar, so planning leave, clashes between two courts on one day, or a client's next date means scrolling a list.

    Fix: a Calendar tab: month grid with hearing counts per day, tap a day for its list, clashes marked. Built from data we already have.

  4. 04

    Two cases per phone screen High

    Home, All cases

    Each case card is about 250 px tall and repeats the date twice (the tile and "Fri, 02 Oct 2026" beside it). A lawyer with 40 cases scrolls 20 screens.

    Fix: compact rows (about 84 px): date or item number once, title, one line of court and stage, status chip.

  5. 05

    Status colours don't mean one thing each High

    Everywhere

    The same red marks "Today", "Urgent" and errors; "High" is orange; "Pending" is green. There's no status for adjourned, no date listed, reserved or judge on leave, so the eye can't sort cases by state.

    Fix: one badge set with a fixed meaning per colour: listed, adjourned, reserved, disposed, overdue, judge on leave; priority shown separately.

  6. 06

    The case page is four screens long, in the wrong order Medium

    Case page

    3,350 px on a phone. Your own notes and the client's name are at the very bottom; orders and hearing history are two separate lists, so you can't see which order came out of which hearing.

    Fix: one timeline of hearings and orders, notes pinned under the title, quick actions (open latest order, add note, share with client) in one row.

  7. 07

    "My cases" bulk import is hidden off-screen Medium

    Add case

    Six tabs scroll sideways and only the first three and a half are visible. You asked for "pick my name, tick several cases, add at once", and that feature already exists under the sixth tab.

    Fix: Add case opens on three big choices (Case number, Screenshot, All my cases); the rest sit under "Other ways".

  8. 08

    Two Add buttons, one covering the content Medium

    Home, All cases

    A floating "+ Add case" sits on top of the bottom bar's "Add". It covers the last case card and the court line of the cards it passes over.

    Fix: keep the Add tab; drop the floating button.

  9. 09

    Adding by case number starts with a wall of courts Medium

    Add case

    The court list fills the first phone screen before the case-number field appears, and the case-type box reads "Loading case types…" as if it were a choice.

    Fix: the last-used court pre-selected as one line with "Change"; case number first; a proper loading state.

  10. 10

    It looks like a template Medium

    Everywhere

    System font, a navy bar, and every block drawn as the same 14 px rounded card with the same shadow. Nothing says "court" or "chambers", and with every block framed alike, nothing stands out.

    Fix: pick one of the directions below and apply it through tokens.

  11. 11

    The header spends its space on the plan counter Low

    Every page

    "8/50 cases" sits in the header on every screen, while search, the thing you reach for in court, is two taps away.

    Fix: search in the header; plan usage moves to Settings.

  12. 12

    Some text is too faint to read in sunlight Medium

    Case list, Settings

    The faint grey used for "No date" and locked features measures about 3.0 : 1 on white, below the 4.5 : 1 that WCAG AA asks for small text. Hard to read on a phone outside a courtroom.

    Fix: every text colour checked at AA in both light and dark themes.

  13. 13

    Two ways of saving on one page Low

    Case page

    Reminders save the moment you flip a switch; notes need a Save button at the bottom of their card. Field spacing in the notes form is uneven (Client name sits apart, Notes sits tight).

    Fix: notes save on their own too, with a quiet "Saved" mark; one form rhythm across the app.

  14. 14

    Waiting looks like nothing is happening Low

    Add case, search, case page

    Searches that take 20 to 60 seconds, the order text being read, and case types loading all show plain text. There are no skeleton rows or progress for long eCourts lookups.

    Fix: skeleton rows, a progress line with what's being checked ("Court 2 of 3"), and an empty state for each list.

  15. 15

    On a laptop it's a phone column in an empty page Low

    Case page, Home at 1280 px

    Content stays one narrow column with two thirds of the screen blank, so chamber work on a laptop gets the phone layout.

    Fix: two columns on wide screens: timeline left, case facts, notes and actions right; Today beside the calendar.

Out of scope for the redesign, as the brief asks

  • Tasks, documents, filings, client accounts and staff roles: these need new data, so they're separate features.
  • The Calendar (problem 03) is the one new screen, at your request. It uses hearing dates we already store.
  • "Judge on leave" needs a small backend change (reading it from eCourts); the redesign reserves its badge.

Why Docket, and what happens next

The audit's top problems are about speed in court: overdue matters hidden, item numbers buried, two cases per screen. Docket answers all three by design: item number first, rows instead of cards, and an attention strip that can't be missed. It stays calm because colour is reserved for state.

You can also mix: Docket's layout with Court Paper's serif for case titles is a reasonable middle.

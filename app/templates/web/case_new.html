Finding your case on eCourts

This can take up to a minute. Please keep this page open.

Where do I find the CNR number?
  1. It's printed at the top of the case status page on eCourts (services.ecourts.gov.in), labelled CNR Number.
  2. It also appears on cause lists and on the first page of court orders.
  3. The first 4 letters are the court code, e.g. DLHC for Delhi High Court.

No CNR? Find the case by party name, case number or FIR.
